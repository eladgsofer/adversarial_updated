# ADVERSARIAL_SENSITIVTY

Welcome to the official repository for the research paper titled "Unveiling and Mitigating Adversarial Vulnerabilities in Iterative Optimizers" authored by Elad Sofer, Tomer Shaked, Caroline Chaux and Nir Shlezinger.

The authors Elad Sofer, Tomer Shaked and Nir Shlezinger are with the School of ECE, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel (e-mail: elad.g.sofer@gmail.com; tosha@post.bgu.ac.il, nirshl@bgu.ac.il) and Caroline Chaux of CNRS, IPAL, Singapore (e-mail caroline.chaux@cnrs.fr).
